**Last Updated:** February 20, 2026

Skill Check ("we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you visit [www.skillcheckgame.com].

**1. Information We Collect**

- **Account Information:** When you create an account, we collect your email address via Supabase magic links.
- **Game Data:** We store your game progress, scores, unlocks, collection data, and run history to provide cloud saves and leaderboards.
- **Gameplay Analytics:** When you complete a run, we log anonymised gameplay data including your display name, scores, stage performance, and question results. This data is used solely to improve game balance and content quality. Your email address may be included in these logs for account identification purposes.
- **User Submissions:** If you submit theme ideas or feedback through the in-game tools, your display name and the content of your submission are logged for review.

**2. Payment Processing**
We do not store your credit card details. All payments are securely processed by our Merchant of Record, **Lemon Squeezy**. You can read their privacy policy at lemonsqueezy.com/privacy.

**3. How We Use Your Data**

- To provide, maintain, and improve the game.
- To display your scores on the global leaderboard.
- To sync your progress across devices.
- To analyse gameplay patterns and improve daily content, difficulty balancing, and question quality.
- To review and potentially feature player-submitted theme ideas in future daily content. If your theme is selected, your display name may be shown to other players as credit.

**4. Contact Us**
If you have questions about this policy, contact us at **support@ki10games.com**.
